Network Desk

Stop treating your network like a contact list. Start treating it like a strategy.

The Real Problem Job Seekers Face

You have 500 LinkedIn connections. You're job searching. You know you should "activate your network," but which 500? Your best friend from university? Your old boss? That person you met once at a conference three years ago?

Most job seekers spray and pray. They send 50 messages hoping 2–3 respond. Network Desk changes this entirely.

How It Works

What Goes In

  • LinkedIn export: Your complete connection history with message threads and interaction timestamps. Network Desk reads who you talk to, how often, and how recently.
  • Target companies: The 3–5 organizations you're actually pursuing. This calibrates the ranking algorithm to your specific search.
  • Weekly availability: Be honest about how many hours you can dedicate. Network Desk sizes your weekly queue to what you can actually execute.

What Comes Out

  • Ranked contact queue: 8–15 people sorted by "actual likelihood to help," not recency or closeness. Each person includes a reason: "works at Shopify + strong recommender" or "hired PMs from your background + recent message."
  • Personalized outreach drafts: Not generic templates. Real messages that reference your relationship history, shared interests, and why they specifically matter for your search.
  • Weekly tracking dashboard: How many people reached out to? Response rate? Meetings scheduled? Referrals made? Where are your wins happening?

Three Concrete Scenarios

Scenario 1: The Career Pivot (Banking → Tech PM)

You spent 5 years in banking. Now you want a PM role at a tech company. Your 500 connections are 60% bankers, 15% university friends, 25% random. A human would reach out to their closest banker friends. Network Desk does the opposite — it surfaces the 12 tech people buried in your extended network (people you connected with years ago, people your banker friends know). These 12 have real optionality in tech. You reach out to exactly those people. Result: 4 conversations, 1 warm intro to a hiring manager, 1 offer discussion.

Scenario 2: The Geographic Move (India → Copenhagen)

You're relocating from India to Copenhagen for a PO role. You have maybe 20 Scandinavian connections scattered across your network. A human would never find them all. Network Desk surfaces every single one, weighted by "likelihood to help someone relocating from South Asia." It learns from patterns: who has hired international talent? Who works at your target companies? Who is in your network's network? Your 20 scattered connections become your ranked top 8. You contact the right people, at the right time, with the right context.

Scenario 3: The Warm Introduction at Scale

Without Network Desk: You reach out to 50 people hoping 5 respond, hoping 1 has a real lead. Success rate: 2–4%. With Network Desk: You reach out to 12 people, each chosen because they have a genuine connection to your goal. Success rate: 40–60%. Same time investment. 10× better outcomes.

Why This Matters

Your network's power isn't in its size. It's in understanding who can actually help you, when, and why. Most job seekers think "more connections = better odds." That's backwards. A 50-person network of the right people beats a 500-person network of random contacts every time.

Network Desk turns LinkedIn from a contact graveyard into a strategic asset. Every outreach becomes intentional. Every conversation becomes warm. Your job search shifts from volume to intelligence.

HYROX 40

Five days to race-ready. Built for iPhone, designed for HYROX athletes.

The Training Challenge

HYROX is precise. 8 km run, 8 workout stations, no pace variation. You need to train at your exact pace, hit your exact splits, and know your segment times down to the second. A paper schedule won't cut it. You need a tool that keeps you in rhythm and shows you whether you're improving week to week.

How HYROX 40 Works

What Goes In

  • Baseline fitness level: Your starting point. Are you training from scratch or coming off another race?
  • Training week number: Which week of your HYROX prep cycle you're in. Week 1 looks different from week 4.

What Comes Out

  • Nested timers: Workout timer (your 8 km run pace), segment timers (each station), rest timers (recovery between stations). Everything synced to your fitness level.
  • Performance scoring: Target pace vs. actual pace. Did you hit your splits? Where did you lose time? Which stations are your weakness?
  • Progress tracking: See how your performance improves week to week. Are your segment times dropping? Is your pace getting tighter?

Built for iPhone, Built for Athletes

HYROX 40 is fast and responsive. It runs offline. No app store friction, no subscriptions, just a tool you trust on race day. Your phone becomes your coach — keeping you in rhythm, showing you your weaknesses, celebrating your improvements.

Danish Learning

Personalized language instruction. Daily lessons that adapt to your level.

The Language Learning Problem

Generic textbooks don't work. They're written for everyone, which means they're written for no one. You're stuck with grammar you already know and vocabulary you'll never use. You need daily lessons that match your exact level and your actual learning goals.

How It Works

What Goes In

  • Your Danish level: Self-assess from A1 (absolute beginner) to B2 (intermediate advanced). Be honest about where you are.
  • Focus areas: Which grammar topics do you want to tackle this week? Which vocabulary themes matter to you? Communication skills you're building toward?
  • Study commitment: How many hours can you dedicate this week? Danish Learning adjusts lesson depth and exercise count accordingly.

What Comes Out

  • Daily lesson: Grammar explanations in plain language, authentic Danish examples from real contexts, vocabulary tied to your focus.
  • Exercises: Written production tasks and listening comprehension. Not busywork. Real language practice.
  • Scored feedback: Your answers are graded immediately with detailed explanations. See exactly where you're weak and why. Gap analysis shows what to focus on next.

Learn Like You Live Here

Content is grounded in Danish daily life. Learn the Danish you actually need — not textbook language, but real communication. Notice the cultural details. Understand not just the words, but how Danes think and express themselves.

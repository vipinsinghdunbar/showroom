How HYROX 40 Works

Precision training for the exact 8.6 km race:

  • Your baseline fitness level sets Week 1 targets (this is your 1RM, 2k row time, running pace)
  • Five workouts (Days 1–5) layer up: strength, skill, speed, race simulation, and active recovery
  • Nested timers keep every segment in rhythm — you see target, actual, and how you compare week-to-week
  • Target vs actual scoring shows exactly where you gain or lose time
  • Week-over-week tracking reveals progress and predicts your race finish time

Designed for athletes who know their fitness level and want to peak in 5 days, not 8 weeks.

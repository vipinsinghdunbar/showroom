Today's Lesson (B1 Level)

Monday, Week 3: Conditional Clauses (Betingelsessætninger) + Job Interview Context

Læring (Forklaring)

Concept Explanation

Conditional clauses describe what happens if something occurs. In Danish, there are three main types:

Type 1 (Real): Present condition, future result

Hvis jeg får jobbet, flytter jeg til København.
(If I get the job, I'll move to Copenhagen.)

Type 2 (Hypothetical): Unlikely or impossible

Hvis jeg var dig, ville jeg søge øl job med højere løn.
(If I were you, I would apply for a job with higher salary.)

Common context: Job interviews often use Type 1 to discuss plans ("If you're hired...") and Type 2 to ask hypothetical questions.

Opgaver (Exercises)

Practice & Production

Exercise 1: Fill in the blanks with correct conditional form.

1. Hvis jeg _____ (have) sommerferie, tager jeg til Sverige.
2. Hvad ville du gøre, hvis du _____ (kunne) flytte hvor som helst?
3. Hvis hun starter i juli, _____ (skulle) hun være klar inden oktober.

Exercise 2: Write two conditional sentences about your dream job.

Example: "If I work at [company], I will [specific skill/outcome]."
